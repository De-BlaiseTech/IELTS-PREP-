# Production Content Policy

The existing `src/data/questionBank.js` content is starter/demo content. Do **not** market or label it as official IELTS material.

For production, every item must be:

1. Original and not copied from official IELTS tests or third-party copyrighted banks.
2. Written to the current IELTS format and task conventions.
3. Attached to the correct test/section/passage or listening transcript.
4. Given an independently checked answer key and explanation where applicable.
5. Checked for ambiguity, duplicate answers, accidental clues, grammar, spelling, and word-limit compliance.
6. Marked `published: true` only after content review.

## Minimum production structure

### Listening
Each complete test has 4 parts and 40 questions. Parts 1–2 use everyday/social contexts; Parts 3–4 use education/training contexts. Recordings are intended to be heard once. Audio, transcript and question sequence must be kept together.

### Reading
Academic and General Training tests have 3 sections and 40 questions. Questions must be linked to the relevant passage and use authentic IELTS-style task formats rather than isolated trivia.

### Writing
Store Academic and General Training tasks separately. Preserve the required minimum word counts and task distinctions.

### Speaking
Store Parts 1–3 separately. Part 2 requires a cue card and planning/speaking timing. Part 3 requires connected discussion and follow-up questions.

## 2,000+ target

The target is more than 2,000 **unique** original questions/tasks. Do not reach the target by repeating prompts with names, numbers or superficial wording changed.

Recommended first production release:
- 800+ Listening questions with complete audio/transcripts for the tests actually published.
- 800+ Reading questions linked to complete passages.
- 200+ Writing tasks/prompts.
- 250+ Speaking prompts/questions.

Content that has no corresponding audio must not be published as a completed Listening test.
